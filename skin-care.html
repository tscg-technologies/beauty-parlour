Gentle Wisdom

Post-Facial Care Tips from Our Specialists

Simple, practical guidance to help keep your skin feeling calm and hydrated after your salon appointment.

💧

Hydrate Generously

Drink plenty of water following your facial to support natural moisture balance and maintain that supple dewy texture.

☀️

Sun Protection

Avoid harsh midday sunlight for 24 hours. Always apply a broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen if stepping outdoors.

🚫

Pause Heavy Makeup

Allow your clean pores to breathe! We recommend skipping heavy foundation powders for at least 6–8 hours after a facial.

🧊

Gentle Cleansing Only

Wash your face only with lukewarm or cool water and a mild non-foaming cleanser on the evening of your appointment.
