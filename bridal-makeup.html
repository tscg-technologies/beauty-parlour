Countdown to Radiance

The Bridal Preparation Timeline

A recommended step-by-step roadmap to look and feel your most relaxed and confident.

1

4 to 6 Weeks Prior: Consultation & Skin Planning

Discuss your bridal outfits, jewelry accents, and preferred makeover vibe. Start bi-weekly hair spa sessions and skin hydration rituals to cultivate a smooth, supple canvas.

2

2 Weeks Prior: Hair Trim & Colour Refresh

Refine your hair layers and perform any subtle ammonia-free root touch-ups or gloss treatments so hair looks vibrant and photographs with multi-dimensional depth.

3

2 to 3 Days Prior: Pre-Bridal Pampering

Undergo gentle body waxing, deluxe pedicure, manicure, and our soothing pre-bridal glow facial. This allows full time for any skin flushing to subside.

4

The Wedding Day: Seamless Execution

Relax in our private bridal dressing chamber. We begin with skin prep, move into your flawless HD application, style your hair with fresh blooms, and finish with immaculate saree or lehenga pinning.

Our Artistry Workflow

The BEAUTY PARLOUR Makeup Process

How we ensure a comfortable, long-lasting, and hygienic makeover experience.

1

Skin Hydration Prep

Every makeup begins with a cooling rose mist, barrier serum, and lightweight moisturizer matched to your skin type.

2

Base Match & HD Veil

3

4

Finishing Mist & Draping
